Biblia Variable

Info

Biblia is a recreation of the lettering engraved in a family Bible of the 1800s: the births, marriages and deaths of one household, written in by hand across generations, and the title page set in a heavy engraved serif.

The drawing keeps the hairlines and swelling stems of the original plate, then lets the stroke grow from Regular to Black on a single axis, so the old page can be set at any weight a new page needs.
